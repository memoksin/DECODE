### 5.2.4 Sprint Retrospective

**What went well**

- Every supervisor meeting has a notes file and it is possible to match each task to the meeting, which makes it easier to follow the progress.
- After the difficulties in Sprint 1 a working way is found to record time. In Sprint 1 most hours in the backlog were estimates; in Sprint 2 every finished task has recorded or reported hours.
- Compared with Sprint 1, there are improvements in the planning of SDD work as it was planned in small tasks of 1-3 hours each.

**What did not go well**

- Hours for some tasks were added late and are approximate.
- Tasks were closed in the last days, and some were left "In Progress" overnight or at the same time as others.
- New tasks from meetings arrived all at once, so the scope grew faster than we closed tasks.
- There are still some tasks moved from previous sprints due to the learning curve of our planning and tracking process in the early sprints.

**Improvements for Sprint 3**

| Problem | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Tasks closed late and some approximate hours | Update a task when work on it ends and record the time spent | Everyone |
| Scope grew after each meeting | Estimate new tasks within one day of a meeting and trim other tasks to fit | Scrum Master |
| Tasks carried over from earlier sprints | Review each carried-over task at Sprint 3 planning and decide to do, split, or drop it | Scrum Master and task owners |
| Uneven workload | Review progress and workload at the mid-sprint team meeting | Everyone |
