### 5.2.3 Sprint Review

- **Goal:** Partly met by 23 October. The purpose, scope, context diagram, external interfaces and software estimation are written. The product functions and use cases are in progress. The related work, UI, ERD, usability, user characteristics and presentation tasks are still open with four days left.
- **Literature:** One member finished the six proposal papers; three members are still reading. The new searches on webcam gaze tracking and on experience classification are in progress.
- **Technical research:** Webcam gaze libraries were compared (DEC-22). The Meet capture research (DEC-16) showed that the Meet Media API is closed to new projects, so an extension could only read the compressed video from the page, and Meet lowers the resolution when bandwidth is low. After the second supervisor meeting (21 October) we moved to an open-source platform and chose MiroTalk SFU (DEC-44). A first measurement showed that a 720p camera is enough for gaze estimation at block level (DEC-45, in progress).
- **Problems:**
  - The platform changed during the sprint, so the Meet-based parts of the SRS drafts must be rewritten.
  - Eight tasks were added after the second meeting (DEC-37–46) without estimates, so the burndown does not show this work.
  - The burndown is flat in week 1 and the curve stays above the ideal line, because most SRS tasks were started late.

### 5.2.4 Sprint Retrospective

**What went well**

- Every meeting has its own notes file with its decisions, so we can trace why a decision changed.
- Short, focused research tasks gave clear decisions (video platform in 0.5 h, camera resolution with a measurement).
- Roles were fixed before the second supervisor meeting.

**What did not go well**

- Time was not logged, so the backlog shows estimates instead of real hours.
- We changed the project management tool from Jira to Linear in the middle of the sprint. For a while, statuses were updated in two places.
- Tasks added during the sprint had no estimates.
- SRS sections were started late, and most of the work is left for the last week.

**Improvements for Sprint 2**

| Problem | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| No real hours | Comment the time spent (`⏱ Xh`) when closing each task | Everyone |
| Two tools | Use Linear only; until all members can use it, the Scrum Master copies status changes from Jira | Mehmet |
| Unestimated new tasks | Estimate every task when it is added | Scrum Master |
| Late start | Split large tasks into parts of at most 3 h and check progress in the mid-sprint team meeting | Everyone |
