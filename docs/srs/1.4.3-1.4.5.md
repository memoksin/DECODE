## 1.4.3 User Characteristics

Draft for DEC-29 (1.4.3), DEC-50 (1.4.4), DEC-51 (1.4.5). Sources: Toplantı 2 (21 Oct), DEC-44 (MiroTalk), DEC-45 (camera), SRS 1.4.2/2.2 draft, DEC-25 draft.

- **Interviewer**
  - Role: HR staff or a technical interviewer who opens an interview session for a position and invites candidates with a link.
  - Programming background: not required. Interviewers may come from HR rather than software development.
  - Eye-tracking knowledge: none expected, so the report must be readable without eye-tracking training.
  - Technical expertise: everyday web use. Logs in to DECODE's web client in a desktop browser, with no installation.
  - Use of results: treats the classification as additional insight, not as a hiring decision.

- **Candidate**
  - Role: a job applicant in a remote technical interview who joins from a link as a guest, with no account and no installation.
  - Programming background: from novice to experienced, which is what DECODE estimates. Must be able to read Java, because the code snippets and trending paths are in Java.
  - Equipment: a laptop or desktop computer with a webcam of at least 720p at 30 fps (1080p recommended), a modern web browser and a stable internet connection.
  - Environment: sits facing the camera in reasonable light and keeps head movement low during calibration and reading.
  - Accessibility: glasses, some eye conditions (e.g. strabismus) or reading difficulties such as dyslexia can lower gaze accuracy or change reading patterns. The candidate can decline eye tracking at the consent step, and the interviewer sees a data quality warning when gaze quality is low.
