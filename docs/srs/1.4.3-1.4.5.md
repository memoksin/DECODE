## 1.4.3 User Characteristics

Draft for DEC-29 (1.4.3), DEC-50 (1.4.4), DEC-51 (1.4.5). Sources: Toplantı 2 (21 Oct), DEC-44 (MiroTalk), DEC-45 (camera), SRS 1.4.2/2.2 draft, DEC-25 draft.

- **Interviewer**
  - Role: HR staff or a technical interviewer who opens an interview session for a position and invites candidates with a link.
  - Programming background: not required, but possible. The interviewer may be HR staff without programming experience or a technical interviewer who programs.
  - Eye-tracking knowledge: none expected, so the report must be readable without eye-tracking training.
  - Interviewers only need everyday web skills, since they log in to DECODE's web client in a desktop browser with no installation required.
  - Interviewers treat the classification as additional insight that supports their judgement, not as a hiring decision.

- **Candidate**
  - Role: a job applicant in a remote technical interview who joins from a link as a guest, with no account and no installation.
  - Candidates range from novice to experienced programmers, and this level is what DECODE estimates. They must be able to read Java, because the code snippets and trending paths are in Java. They need a laptop or desktop computer with a webcam of at least 720p at 30 fps (1080p recommended), a modern web browser and a stable internet connection. During calibration and reading, they should sit facing the camera in reasonable light and keep head movement to a minimum. Glasses, certain eye conditions such as strabismus and reading difficulties such as dyslexia may affect gaze accuracy and reading patterns. Candidates can therefore decline eye tracking at the consent stage, and if they take part, the interviewer sees a data quality warning whenever gaze quality is low.
