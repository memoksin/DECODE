## 2.3 Usability Requirements

Draft for DEC-47, based on Burcu's draft in `docs/DEC 25.md`. Consistent with 1.4.3.

- **UR-01 Effectiveness (task completion rate):** At least 90% of interviewers shall complete the core task without help: select a code snippet, open an interview session, send the link to the candidate and open the candidate's report.
  *Justification:* Interviewers may be HR staff without programming or eye-tracking knowledge (1.4.3), and 90% is above the 78% average completion rate reported across usability studies [1].

- **UR-02 Efficiency (calibration time):** Candidates shall complete the 9-point gaze calibration, including the validation point, in under 45 seconds.
  *Justification:* A short calibration avoids delaying the interview and limits the head movement that lowers gaze accuracy (1.4.3).

- **UR-03 Satisfaction (SUS score):** Interviewers shall rate the classification report with a System Usability Scale (SUS) score of at least 75 (grade B).
  *Justification:* The report must be understood without eye-tracking training (1.4.3), and 75 is clearly above the average SUS score of 68 [2].
